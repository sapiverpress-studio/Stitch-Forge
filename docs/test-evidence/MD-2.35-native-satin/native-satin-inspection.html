Native satin engine evidence

Synthetic control only. No AI reconstruction, detailed artwork acceptance or physical fabric sew-out is represented here.

What this verifies

  • Native Ink/Stitch 3.3.0 generated both PES and DST from the same SVG rails and rungs.
  • pyembroidery 1.5.1 independently decoded both files; needle counts and physical extents agree.
  • Decoded needle movements repeatedly cross the requested 4 mm column width and remain within the 60 × 4 mm bounds.
  • PES records a resolved palette thread; arbitrary source RGB is not preserved exactly. DST has no full thread-colour identity.

It does not prove machine compatibility, thread handling, underlay quality, fabric compensation or embroidery quality.
